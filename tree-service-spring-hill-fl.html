What is Tree Service?

Tree service covers the day-to-day and emergency work involved in keeping trees on a property safe, healthy, and appropriately sized for the space around them. This includes tree care practices such as pruning for structure and airflow, removing dead or hazardous trees, grinding stumps down below grade, and clearing storm damage. The work draws on arboriculture, the study and practice of caring for individual trees, and is typically carried out by a trained arborist who understands tree biology, root systems, and the risks tied to different tree diseases and structural weaknesses. Good tree care protects your home, your power lines, and your neighbors' property while keeping trees on your lot healthy longer. For homeowners in Spring Hill, this matters because of the local mix of oak, pine, and palm trees, many of which grew up close to houses, driveways, and septic lines. A qualified arborist looks at root spread, canopy weight, and soil drainage before recommending removal, trimming, or ongoing care, so decisions are based on the actual condition of the tree rather than guesswork.
